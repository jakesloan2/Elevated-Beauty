Our Courses

Everything we teach

Five specialist courses, each designed to give you a complete, confident foundation in your chosen discipline.

Brows · Advanced

Signature & Hybrid Brow Course

Master both microblading and powder/ombre techniques in one comprehensive course, giving you the versatility to offer a full range of brow treatments.

  • Microblading fundamentals and mapping
  • Ombre and powder shading
  • Colour theory and pigment selection
  • Live model practice
  • Aftercare and client management

Brows · Beginner–Intermediate

Brow Lamination

Learn the full brow lamination process — from consultation and solution application through to tinting and aftercare.

  • Full lamination process step-by-step
  • Brow tinting and shaping
  • Contraindications and patch testing
  • Pricing and business guidance

Lashes · Beginner–Intermediate

Lash Lift & Tint

A practical, results-driven course covering the complete lash lift and tint process. Ideal as a standalone offering or add-on to an existing business.

  • Lash lift application and timing
  • Tinting technique and safety
  • Client consultation skills
  • Aftercare and retail guidance

Brows · Comprehensive

The Ultimate Brow Course

The most complete brow training at Elevated Beauty Academy. Covers lamination, sculpting, tinting, and henna.

  • Lamination, sculpt, and tint
  • Henna brows technique
  • Brow mapping for all face shapes
  • Hands-on model practice
  • Business and pricing support

Brows & Lashes · Bundle

The Ultimate Brow & Lash Course

Our most popular package: everything in the Ultimate Brow Course plus a full Lash Lift & Tint qualification.

  • All Ultimate Brow Course content
  • Full Lash Lift & Tint certification
  • Combined kit and materials
  • Ongoing post-course support
  • Certificate of completion
