Booking FAQs

Everything you need to know before your appointment.

How do I know which artist to book with?
Eve specialises in PMU — microblading, powder brows, lip blush, and eyeliner. Ellie specialises in lash extensions, lash lifts, brow lamination, and brow tinting. If you're unsure, email us and we'll help.
Do I need a patch test before my appointment?
Yes. A patch test is required at least 48 hours before your first lash or tint treatment. PMU clients attend a consultation prior to treatment.
What should I do to prepare for my appointment?
Arrive with a clean face, free from makeup around the treatment area. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before PMU. Full prep instructions are sent once your booking is confirmed.
How long does a typical appointment take?
Lash sets typically take 1.5–2.5 hours; PMU appointments 2–3 hours including consultation. Check the individual booking pages for specific durations.
Can I contact the studio directly?
Of course — email us at evevatedbeauty2024@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
